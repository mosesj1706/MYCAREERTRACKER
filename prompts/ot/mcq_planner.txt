About this exam: {exam_style}

- Each concept is one specific, testable point of entry-level OT practice: a condition and the decision it calls
  for, an assessment and when to use it, a precaution, a principle of a frame of reference, a piece of anatomy that
  matters clinically. Name it in under 15 words.
- Every concept must differ from the others and from every question already in her bank (listed in <bank>). A
  different condition, stage, setting, age group or decision counts as different; rewording the same point does not.
- Spread widely across each topic: common and less common conditions, the age groups the topic covers, assessment,
  intervention, precautions and outcomes. The few classic questions that every question set repeats are fine once,
  only if the bank does not already have them.
- Only well-established content; nothing that varies between countries or guidelines.
- topic must be one of the given topics, verbatim.
